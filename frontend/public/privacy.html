Privacy notice

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Pathway stores the account details supplied through Keycloak, learning progress, submitted answers and code, saved project files, assessment results, coaching prompts, and community posts or replies. This data is used only to operate and improve the learning experience, protect the service, and provide support.

Code submissions are sent to an isolated evaluation environment. Do not submit passwords, API keys, personal data, or other secrets. Coaching requests screen for common credentials before provider processing; provider-backed coaching is disabled unless configured by the operator.

We do not sell personal data. We disclose data only to the infrastructure providers needed to operate Pathway, or when required by law.

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your account data through support. This notice must be reviewed and completed by the operator with its legal entity, address, retention periods, and jurisdiction before a public launch.
